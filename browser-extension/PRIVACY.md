# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2024

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Smart Fake Profile Detection browser extension ("we", "our", or "the extension") collects, uses, and protects your information.

## Information Collection and Usage

### What We Collect

The extension may collect the following types of information:

1. **Profile Data**: When you visit social media profiles, the extension may extract publicly available profile information including:
   - Username
   - Follower/following counts
   - Number of posts
   - Bio length
   - Profile picture presence
   - Other publicly visible profile characteristics

2. **Usage Data**: Basic information about how you use the extension, such as:
   - Which social media platforms you visit
   - When you use the extension features
   - Settings preferences (stored locally)

### How We Use Information

- **Profile Analysis**: Collected profile data is used solely for the purpose of detecting potentially fake profiles.
- **Local Processing**: All analysis happens either in your browser or on your own local server.
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: We do not share, sell, or transfer any of your data with third parties.

## Data Storage

### Browser Storage

- Extension settings and preferences are stored locally in your browser using Chrome Storage API.
- This data never leaves your device.

### Local Server

- If you use the integration with the local Fake Profile Detection server:
  - Data is sent only to your own server running on `http://localhost:5000`
  - No data is transmitted to external servers
  - You control your own server and data

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:
- All data stays on your local device or your own server
- No internet connection to external services
- encryption for sensitive information is not stored

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the extension at any time
- Delete all local data by uninstalling the extension
- Control what profile data is processed by disabling the extension

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the project repository.
